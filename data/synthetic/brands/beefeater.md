# Beefeater Gin

Beefeater is a London Dry gin distilled in London and part of the Pernod Ricard portfolio. The recipe is built on juniper and a botanical bill that typically includes citrus peel, angelica, orris, and coriander, producing a crisp, juniper-forward gin.

The classic Beefeater serve is a gin and tonic with a citrus garnish. The gin also appears in a Dry Martini and a Tom Collins. All cocktail suggestions assume legal drinking age and moderate consumption. Do not provide medical advice about alcohol.

The official site is https://www.beefeatergin.com. This knowledge base refuses competitor rankings against other gin producers.
