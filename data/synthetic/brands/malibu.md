# Malibu

Malibu is a Caribbean rum liqueur with coconut flavour, owned by Pernod Ricard. It is commonly used in tropical mixed drinks such as Malibu and pineapple, and in longer serves with soda or citrus.

Malibu should be enjoyed by adults of legal drinking age only. Cocktail descriptions must include a responsible-drinking reminder and must never include prices or purchase instructions. The official brand website is https://www.maliburumdrinks.com.

Kahlúa, a coffee liqueur also in the Pernod Ricard portfolio, is a separate brand used in coffee-based cocktails such as the Espresso Martini and the White Russian. Both brands are presented independently, without competitive comparison to products outside the group.
