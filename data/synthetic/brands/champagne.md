# G.H. Mumm and Perrier-Jouët

G.H. Mumm and Perrier-Jouët are Champagne houses in the Pernod Ricard wine and champagne portfolio. G.H. Mumm is associated with Cordon Rouge and with ceremonial moments in motorsport and celebration. Perrier-Jouët is associated with floral Art Nouveau styling and the Belle Epoque cuvées.

Champagne is an appellation-controlled sparkling wine from the Champagne region of France. Serves are typically well chilled in a flute or tulip glass. Sparkling wine, like all alcohol, should be consumed responsibly by adults of legal drinking age.

Official sites include https://www.ghmumm.com and https://www.perrier-jouet.com. This knowledge base does not quote retail prices or recommend retailers.
