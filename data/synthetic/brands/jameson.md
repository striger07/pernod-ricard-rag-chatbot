# Jameson Irish Whiskey

Jameson is a triple-distilled Irish whiskey produced in Ireland and belonging to the Pernod Ricard brand portfolio. The classic Jameson bottle is widely used in mixed drinks, notably the whiskey ginger and the Irish coffee, as well as being sipped neat or over ice.

The whiskey is generally characterised as smooth and approachable, with notes of vanilla, toasted wood, and a light spicy finish. Jameson Caskmates and aged expressions extend the core range. Serve suggestions must never encourage excessive consumption.
