# The Glenlivet

The Glenlivet is a single malt Scotch whisky distillery in Speyside, Scotland, and a strategic brand in the Pernod Ricard portfolio. Founded in the nineteenth century, the distillery is associated with a fruity, elegant Speyside style.

The Glenlivet 12 Year Old is often described with notes of citrus, pineapple, and vanilla. The 15 Year Old French Oak and 18 Year Old expressions explore deeper oak influence. Single malts are commonly tasted neat or with a few drops of water to open the aroma.

Tasting notes are descriptive, not medical claims. Whisky should only be consumed by adults of legal drinking age, in moderation. Visit https://www.theglenlivet.com for official brand information.
