# Chivas Regal

Chivas Regal is a blended Scotch whisky in the Pernod Ricard portfolio. The brand traces its origins to the Chivas brothers’ grocery and wine business in Aberdeen, Scotland, in the nineteenth century. The 12-year-old expression is among the world’s best-known blended Scotches.

Chivas Regal 12 is typically described as a smooth, honeyed blend with notes of ripe orchard fruit and a gentle oak finish. It can be served neat, with a splash of water, or over ice. Highballs with soda water are a common long drink. Always drink responsibly and never drink and drive.
