# Absolut Vodka

Absolut is a Swedish vodka brand in the Pernod Ricard portfolio. Production is associated with Åhus in southern Sweden. The brand is known for continuous distillation, a distinctive bottle silhouette, and long-running creative campaigns in art and design.

Absolut vodka is typically enjoyed neat, over ice, or as a base in mixed drinks. Classic serves include the Absolut and tonic, the Absolut vodka soda, and the Cosmopolitan when combined with citrus liqueur and cranberry. All serves should be enjoyed in moderation by adults of legal drinking age.
