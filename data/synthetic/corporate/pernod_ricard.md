# Pernod Ricard

Pernod Ricard is a global leader in wines and spirits, created in 1975 through the merger of Pernod and Ricard. The group builds, markets, and distributes a portfolio of international premium brands across whiskies, vodka, gin, rum, liqueurs, cognac, and champagne.

The company is headquartered in Paris, France. Its stated ambition is to be a créateur de convivialité: a creator of conviviality. That purpose is bounded by a public commitment to responsible drinking, marketing codes, and age-gating of alcohol-related communications.

## Portfolio

The group’s strategic international brands include Absolut vodka, Chivas Regal and The Glenlivet Scotch whiskies, Jameson Irish whiskey, Beefeater gin, Ballantine’s, Royal Salute, Malibu, Kahlúa, G.H. Mumm, and Perrier-Jouët. Local and specialty brands complement the international portfolio in individual markets.

## Responsible drinking

Pernod Ricard does not provide product prices, purchasing instructions, or medical advice through this knowledge base. Cocktail and serve suggestions must be accompanied by a responsible-drinking reminder. Questions about health, addiction, or alcohol safety should be directed to independent organisations such as Drinkaware and the U.S. National Institute on Alcohol Abuse and Alcoholism (NIAAA).
